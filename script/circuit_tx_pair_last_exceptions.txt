# THE LIST IS A RATCHET, NOT AN AMNESTY. Every entry here is a circuit with the pair in the

# the circuit alone yields a proof that fails verification, and
# `scripts/check-circuit-metadata-alignment.sh` is the instrument that says so — it compares
