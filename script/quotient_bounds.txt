# Quotient-bound operands (OBL-Z12).
#
# The Lean theorem `qr_unique` (`proofs/lean/src/DarkFi/BaseDivGadget.lean`) gives quotient and
# remainder uniqueness only over integers. A circuit that forms a quotient from field elements
# without establishing their integer bound proves a statement about field residues instead.
# `range_check` is the on-chain establishment of that bound.
#
# Format: <circuit path> : <operand> <operand> ...
# A new quotient-remainder circuit is added here in the same change; the gate fails if a listed
# operand loses its range check.
