# Recommended Books

> These texts inform the intellectual context of the DarkFi/DarkWow lineage.
> Inclusion does not imply endorsement. See [Philosophy](philosophy.md) for
> DarkWow's architectural design rationale.

## Technical Books

### Mathematics

* Pinter, *A Book of Abstract Algebra*. Fundamental algebra text.
* Dummit & Foote, *Algebra*. Comprehensive reference.
* Washington, *Elliptic Curves: Number Theory and Cryptography*. Computational approach.
* Silverman, *The Arithmetic of Elliptic Curves*. Definitive text.
* Atiyah-MacDonald, *Introduction to Commutative Algebra*.
* Jarvis, *Algebraic Number Theory*, chapters 1-5. Ideal for self-study.

### Cryptography

* [Proofs, Arguments, and Zero-Knowledge](https://people.cs.georgetown.edu/jthaler/ProofsArgsAndZK.pdf) by Justin Thaler.

### Programming

* *The C Programming Language* by Kernighan & Ritchie.
* *The Rust Programming Language* (No Starch Press).
* *Rust for Rustaceans* (No Starch Press).
* *Python Crash Course* by Eric Matthes.

### Economics

* Werner, *New Paradigm in Macroeconomics*. How economics and finance work at a fundamental level.
* [Cryptoeconomics](https://voskuil.org/cryptoeconomics/index.html) by Eric Voskuil.

### Technology

* [Authoritarian vs Democratic Technics](https://wiki.polytech.barcelona/index.php?title=File:Mumford_authoritarian_vs_democratic_technics.pdf) by Lewis Mumford.
* [GNU and Free Software texts](https://www.gnu.org/philosophy/philosophy.html).

## Historical Context

These texts inform the intellectual lineage of the fork. Read as context,
not as endorsement.

* Mark Fisher, [*Capitalist Realism: Is There No Alternative?*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitalist_Realism) (2009).
* Mark Fisher, [*Ghosts of My Life: Writings on Depression, Hauntology and Lost Futures*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghosts_of_My_Life) (2014).
* Nick Land, [*Crypto-Current: Bitcoin and Philosophy*](https://etscrivner.github.io/cryptocurrent/) (2018) — The essential bridge text on chronogenesis.
* Nick Land, [*Fanged Noumena*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fanged_Noumena). Read alongside Fisher.
* David Graeber, [*Debt: The First 5,000 Years*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debt:_The_First_5,000_Years) (2011).
* [Warwick CCRU Collection](https://app.ardrive.io/#/drives/1c3b923e-eba9-402f-862a-e532c1df53fd?name=Warwick+CCRU) on ArDrive — both Fisher and Land texts.
