# Ideology

DarkWow's ideology is inseparable from its technical architecture. Where DarkFi
upstream inherits the Dark Enlightenment trajectory of Nick Land —
governance-DAO plutocracy, premine extraction, SAFT financialization —
DarkWow follows the left-accelerationist trajectory of Mark Fisher:
technology repurposed toward disintermediation and temporal sovereignty.
Same technical base (zkVM, Halo2, WASM runtime, P2P stack); opposite
political conclusion. See the [Philosophy](philosophy.md) page for the
full articulation.

## Core Principles

1. **No Governance DAO — Pure PoW**: Consensus derives from work, not
   token-holder voting. This rejects the plutocratic premise of governance
   tokens — and with it, capitalist realism's core claim that "there is
   no alternative" to hierarchical control. The governance token is
   capitalist realism made into a smart contract: it assumes that someone
   must be in charge, that decisions must be financialized, and that voting
   power should be proportional to wealth. DarkWow removes the governance
   surface entirely. There is nothing to capture because there is no DAO.

2. **No Premine — Every Coin Mined**: The coin supply originates entirely
   from proof of work. No founder allocation, no VC tranche, no "fair
   launch" theater. A premine colonizes the future by allocating tokens to
   insiders before the chain begins — it is the temporal equivalent of
   enclosure. Zero premine is temporal sovereignty: the chain starts when
   the first miner finds a block, not when insiders unlock. Cyberspacetime
   reclaimed for miners and users.

3. **Sovereignty Through Cryptography**: ZK proofs provide the technical
   substrate for sovereignty — the ability to prove a statement is true
   without revealing the underlying information. This is left-accelerationist
   in its structure: the technology enables coordination without the
   surrender of identity, collective action without the vulnerability of
   public exposure.

4. **O-Cap Authorization**: Object capabilities replace access control lists.
   This is the architectural expression of post-capitalist desire.
   Capabilities are granted, not accumulated. A contract cannot accumulate
   authority the way capital accumulates interest — it holds only what was
   explicitly passed to it. The architecture forecloses the concentration
   of power by making it structurally inexpressible. See
   [O-Cap & Composable Privacy](../arch/ocap.md).

5. **AI-Assisted Development as Design Principle**: DarkWow's architecture
   is intentionally AI-friendly. O-Cap authorization means AI-generated
   contracts cannot violate capability boundaries. Deterministic Uncle Merkle
   consensus means tests are reproducible — same code, same result, every
   time. The four-level test pipeline provides a safety net that catches
   AI-generated errors before mainnet. This is left-accelerationist praxis:
   embracing AI to accelerate development beyond centralized gatekeepers,
   with the O-Cap boundary ensuring that acceleration does not reproduce
   the hierarchies of capitalist control. Complex smart contracts can be
   vibe-coded because the architecture constrains what can go wrong —
   acceleration with a safety net.
